SKIN HEALTH & HYGIENE

Healthy skin starts with simple care.

Your skin helps protect your body. A gentle, consistent routine can support its protective barrier. Choose products that suit your skin, avoid harsh scrubbing, and seek medical advice for persistent skin concerns.

1

Cleanse gently

Use a mild cleanser and lukewarm water. Avoid rough scrubbing.

2

Moisturise

Choose a moisturiser that fits your skin and helps prevent dryness.

3

Protect from the sun

Consider broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen and follow the label instructions.

Skin health tip: Patch-test new products as directed. Stop using products that cause irritation. For a persistent rash, painful acne, changing mole or signs of infection, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

DISEASE PREVENTION

Reduce risks. Protect yourself.

Learn practical prevention habits for common health risks.

🫁

Respiratory Infections

Help reduce transmission by maintaining hand hygiene, improving ventilation and staying home when unwell.

  • Wash hands regularly
  • Cover coughs and sneezes
  • Avoid close contact when sick
💧

Food & Water Safety

Safe food handling and clean drinking water are important parts of infection prevention.

  • Use safe drinking water
  • Wash fresh produce
  • Keep raw and cooked foods separate
🦟

Mosquito-Borne Diseases

Reduce mosquito exposure and remove standing water around living areas.

  • Use mosquito protection
  • Wear suitable clothing
  • Remove standing water
💉

Vaccination

Keep vaccinations up to date according to recommended schedules and professional advice.

  • Check vaccination records
  • Follow recommended schedules
  • Ask a healthcare professional questions

How to wash your hands

  1. Wet your hands with clean running water.
  2. Apply soap and create a good lather.
  3. Scrub palms, backs of hands, between fingers and under fingernails.
  4. Scrub for at least 20 seconds.
  5. Rinse thoroughly under clean running water.
  6. Dry your hands completely.
🪥

Oral hygiene tips

  • Brush your teeth regularly.
  • Keep your toothbrush clean.
  • Pay attention to your gum health.
  • Have regular dental checkups as appropriate.
  • Seek professional advice for persistent dental problems.
🚿

Daily body hygiene

  • Bathe regularly according to your needs.
  • Keep skin folds clean and dry.
  • Wear clean clothes.
  • Keep towels and personal items clean.
  • Wash hands after using the toilet.
👕

Clean clothing habits

  • Change sweaty or dirty clothes regularly.
  • Wash towels and bedding regularly.
  • Allow damp clothes to dry properly.
  • Avoid sharing personal hygiene items.
